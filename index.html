01 / MANIFESTO

MUSIC
IS NOT
CONTENT.

A melody can change a room, a memory, a night — sometimes an entire moment of a life.

At PHBH MUSIC, technology is never the point. Emotion is. A classical guitar, a piano, a synthesizer or an AI system are instruments in different forms. What matters is the intention behind the sound and the responsibility taken when it is released into the world.

We do not publish music because a machine can make it. We publish music when there is something worth transmitting.

05 / THE LABEL

INDEPENDENT · VIENNA · AUSTRIA

PHBH MUSIC

A small label by design. Close to the music, close to the people behind it.

PHBH MUSIC develops and releases independent artists with a focus on electronic music, lyric-driven concepts and contemporary AI-assisted production. The label is directed from Vienna by Philipp Brussmann-Hablich.

We use modern tools to expand expression, not to remove the human decision from it. The final question is never “Can we release this?” It is “Should this exist — and what does it say once it does?”
